# Hi, I'm Furqan Khan

I'm a management consultant. I make slides. Big slides. Important slides. And one day I'm sitting there thinking, "Man, I tell people how to run their business all day... and I can't even build a to-do app."

So I got AI to teach me. No coding background. None. Zero. I thought Python was a snake. But everybody says AI's coming for my job, and I'd like to stay employed. So I'm learning how it works. Gotta know your replacement.

But here's the thing about AI. It's confident. Real confident. It'll write you a thousand lines of code and look you dead in the eye like, "Yeah, that works." It does not work.

So I did what any consultant would do. I hired a team to check its work.

That team is **[Olympus](https://github.com/khanfurqan/Olympus-AI-Coding-Harness)**. Like the gods. AI specialists that plan, review and explain the code. I built a whole committee to supervise the robot. That's not engineering. That's management. That's the only thing I know how to do!

**What the Olympians got me building:**
- **[Sibyl](https://github.com/khanfurqan/sibyl)**: you talk, it cleans up what you said.
- **An investment research terminal**, because I plan on being rich. Eventually.
- **A gym app** that tells me what to do. Same as everybody else in my life.
- **Financial-services tools**, for the industry I advise. Day job sneaking into the night job.

Why? Because I'm tired of paying subscriptions. Every app wants $19.99 a month. Forever. For dictation. Nah. I'll build it myself. Badly. But it's mine.

**How I work:** I write boring code. On purpose. 'Cause six months from now, Future Me has to understand it, and Future Me is not smarter than me. I been watching him.
